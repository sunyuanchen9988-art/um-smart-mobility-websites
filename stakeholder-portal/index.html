FAQ

Questions evaluators are likely to ask

Why not copy Copenhagen or Harvard directly?

UM needs adaptation for tropical humidity, monsoon rain, 22% gradients, campus governance, and Malaysian finance instruments.

Why is the digital layer delivered first?

Baseline data, PMIS controls, and public evidence must exist before major vehicle and civil-work spending begins.

How is equity protected?

B40 verification, free or discounted rates, sheltered motorcycle parking, and staged parking reform reduce transition risk.
