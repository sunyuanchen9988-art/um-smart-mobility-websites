Safety and accessibility

Inclusive by default, not as an afterthought

Accessibility mode

Wheelchair-accessible route preference, low-vision UI, bigger controls, and routes that avoid steep ramps where possible.

Emergency call

One-tap UM Security call with location shared, suitable for night travel and incident reporting.

B40-rate verification

Eligibility linked to Bursary records so discounted fares appear automatically for eligible users.

Feedback

One-tap reports for daily service improvement

Operations response standard

  • Critical safety reports routed immediately to UM Security.
  • Mobility asset faults assigned to the operator queue.
  • Monthly feedback themes published to the Stakeholder Portal.
